Learning **Blender** from scratch on a Mac? This is the full beginner curriculum — 7 hands-on lessons (about 8 hours total), in the order that actually makes sense, from the interface to lighting and rendering.

Each lesson is a real, do-it-yourself walkthrough. For hands-free help, [Skilly](/) teaches this exact curriculum live — a voice tutor that watches your Blender window and points your cursor at the right button. Free to start.

## The Blender curriculum

1. **[3D Viewport Navigation](/learn/blender-3d-viewport-navigation)** — Learn to move around the 3D Viewport — the foundation for everything that follows.
2. **[Object Mode and the Scene Hierarchy](/learn/blender-object-mode-and-the-scene-hierarchy)** — Understand how objects, collections, and the Outliner work — before touching any mesh geometry.
3. **[Edit Mode and Mesh Geometry](/learn/blender-edit-mode-and-mesh-geometry)** — Cross the critical threshold from moving objects to editing their geometry.
4. **[Core Mesh Editing Tools](/learn/blender-core-mesh-editing-tools)** — Build real 3D shapes using the five essential mesh editing operations.
5. **[Modifiers](/learn/blender-modifiers)** — Apply non-destructive operations that reshape the mesh without permanently changing the underlying geometry.
6. **[Materials and Shading](/learn/blender-materials-and-shading)** — Give the mesh a surface appearance using the Principled BSDF shader and the Shader Editor.
7. **[Lighting and Rendering](/learn/blender-lighting-and-rendering)** — Set up a scene with proper lighting and produce a final rendered image.

## How to use this

Work top to bottom — each lesson builds on the last — or jump straight to what you're stuck on. Every page lists what you'll do, the exact menus and shortcuts, and the common mistakes to avoid.
