The site is crawlable and technically healthy across the 50 pages reviewed. The main opportunity is organic traction: Search Console shows 1,461 impressions for “skilly” in three months but only 0.3% click-through, while the domain has an estimated 17 organic visits and 30 ranking keywords. This review covers the crawl, metadata, branded demand, and the first practical improvement.

Where the site stands

The crawl completed all 50 pages, every crawled page returned HTTP 200, and the pages were indexable. There were no critical or warning issues.

The remaining issues are informational: 23 long titles, two long meta descriptions, and one heading-order skip. The bigger growth constraint is visibility and click-through, not a broken technical foundation.

If you only do one thing

Make the homepage the clearest search result for the Skilly product by fixing its heading order and tightening its product-focused title and opening copy.

Search Console recorded 1,461 impressions for “skilly” at an average position of 7.7 but only 5 clicks, a 0.3% click-through rate. The homepage currently starts with an H2, “Skilly for Builders,” before its H1, “Let users ask your product what to do next.”

  1. Make “Skilly for Builders” a non-heading eyebrow or move it below the H1 so the page begins with one clear H1.
  2. Rewrite the homepage title and first paragraph around the product category, for example: “Skilly: Voice onboarding for SaaS and apps.”
  3. Deploy, then compare branded impressions and click-through rate in Search Console after 28 days.

Small fixes, in order

Homepage heading order info

Live HTML starts with <h2>Skilly for Builders</h2> and then <h1>Let users ask your product what to do next.</h1>.

Fix: Keep one H1 as the first page heading. Render the brand label as text or a styled paragraph.

Learn-page titles info

OpenSEO found 23 titles above its suggested range. Live examples include Best voice AI assistants for Mac in 2026 (honest, grouped by use case) — Skilly at 79 characters and Houdini Caching, Versioning, and Production Hygiene (beginner guide) — Skilly at 77.

Fix: Shorten the 23 titles to roughly 50–60 characters, keeping the query topic first and moving “Skilly” to the end only when space allows.

Meta descriptions info

Two descriptions exceed the audit range. Live examples are 165 characters on the voice-assistant guide and 161 on the Figma Auto Layout tutorial.

Fix: Trim each to about 140–155 characters, preserving the topic, audience, and a reason to click.

Backlink depth info

OpenSEO recorded 24 backlinks from 14 referring domains. The largest source is stackscope.dev with eight backlinks; the profile is still small relative to the number of indexable pages.

Fix: Promote one useful product-led asset, such as an onboarding benchmark or interactive demo, to relevant SaaS, developer-tool, and product-design communities.

Where to focus first

Build around product-led brand and onboarding intent before adding more broad AI content. Search Console currently gives the clearest evidence for the branded queries below; these are first-party impressions, not estimated monthly search volume.

This is the strongest demand signal and already averages position 7.7.
