Learning **DaVinci Resolve** from scratch on a Mac? This is the full beginner curriculum — 7 hands-on lessons (about 7 hours total), in the order that actually makes sense, from the interface to deliver and export.

Each lesson is a real, do-it-yourself walkthrough. For hands-free help, [Skilly](/) teaches this exact curriculum live — a voice tutor that watches your DaVinci Resolve window and points your cursor at the right button. Free to start.

## The DaVinci Resolve curriculum

1. **[Interface and Pages](/learn/davinci-resolve-interface-and-pages)** — Understand DaVinci Resolve's unique 7-page architecture — the single most important concept for any new user.
2. **[Media Import](/learn/davinci-resolve-media-import)** — Bring footage into your project and organize it in the Media Pool before you start editing.
3. **[Edit Page Basics](/learn/davinci-resolve-edit-page-basics)** — Assemble your rough cut on the Edit page using the full-featured editing tools.
4. **[Trimming and Transitions](/learn/davinci-resolve-trimming-and-transitions)** — Refine your edit with precise trimming tools and add transitions between clips.
5. **[Color Grading Basics](/learn/davinci-resolve-color-grading-basics)** — Move to the Color page to correct and enhance the look of your footage using DaVinci's professional grading tools.
6. **[Audio and Fairlight](/learn/davinci-resolve-audio-and-fairlight)** — Move to the Fairlight page for professional audio mixing, EQ, and loudness monitoring.
7. **[Deliver and Export](/learn/davinci-resolve-deliver-and-export)** — Render your finished project to a file using the Deliver page.

## How to use this

Work top to bottom — each lesson builds on the last — or jump straight to what you're stuck on. Every page lists what you'll do, the exact menus and shortcuts, and the common mistakes to avoid.
