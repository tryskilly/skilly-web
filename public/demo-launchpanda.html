❓FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LaunchPanda?+
A free tool that helps indie builders find the best directories to submit their product to. Save your product info once, get matched to the right directories, and copy-paste your way to launch day. We also actively track and add the latest directories so you never miss a new place to launch.
Is it really free?+
Yes! Browsing directories, saving your Startup Profile, copy-pasting fields, and tracking your progress is completely free. Just sign in with your Google account. Pro features (unlimited profiles, launch roadmap, submission tracker) are available as a monthly subscription or one-time lifetime purchase.
Why should I submit to directories?+
Three reasons: backlinks (free SEO that helps you rank on Google), audience (thousands of early adopters browsing daily), and feedback (honest input from people who actually build things).
How long does it take?+
About 30 seconds to save your profile. Then 1-2 minutes per directory submission (copy, open, paste). The whole process for 140+ directories takes about 4 hours instead of the usual 40+ hours.
How is this better than a spreadsheet?+
A spreadsheet doesn't pre-fill your info, match directories to your product, show difficulty ratings, let you copy with one click, or track what you've completed. We turn a 3-hour copy-paste marathon into a 30-minute focused session.
Is my data safe?+
Your Startup Profile is stored in your browser. We never share your data with third parties. We only use your Google account to save your profile and send occasional launch tips (unsubscribe anytime).
Do you offer a money-back guarantee on Auto Launch?+
Yes. If you're not happy with your Auto Launch within 30 days of purchase, we'll refund you in full, no questions asked. Heads-up: when we issue the refund we revert your launches across the directories we submitted to, and the dofollow backlinks from those listings are lost. So you keep the dofollows if you keep the order, no compromise either way.
